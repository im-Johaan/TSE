Which speaker does the output sound like?

SI-SDR in dB, higher is better. Roughly: above 10 dB is clean, 5–10 dB has audible leakage from the other voices, below 0 dB means the extraction failed or picked the wrong person. Improvement = output SI-SDR − input (mixture) SI-SDR.
